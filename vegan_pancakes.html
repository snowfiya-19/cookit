Vegan Pancakes Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 All-Purpose Flour
  • 2 Baking powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 Sugar
  • 4 Non-Dairy Milk & Apple Cider Vinegar
  • 5 Oil
  • 6 Vegan Butter & Oil
  • Water
  • Optional toppings:
    • Vegan chocolate, peanut butter and slices of banana
    • Whipped coconut cream and strawberries
    • Maple syrup
    • Fresh berries

Instructions

  1. Step 1: SIFT the flour and baking powder into a large mixing bowl. Add the sugar and salt, and whisk to combine.
  2. Step 2: make vegan buttermilk by pouring the vinegar into the non-dairy milk. Set aside to curdle.
  3. Step 3: Add the melted vegan butter, oil, and vanilla to the buttermilk mixture, and whisk to combine.
  4. Step 4: Pour the wet ingredients into the mixing bowl with the dry ingredients.
  5. Step 5: Ladle slightly less than ⅓ cup of pancake batter onto the heated griddle or into the pan. When the edges begin to set and you see bubbles forming on top of the pancake, it's ready to flip.
  6. Step 6: Flip the pancakes using a wide spatula, and continue to cook for another minute or until the center bounces back when pressed.
  7. Assemble the pancake: Spread the mashed avocado evenly over the toasted bread.
  8. Add Toppings: Top with your choice of berries, maple syrup, whipped cream, or Mango compote.
  9. Serve: Enjoy immediately as a delicious breakfast, snack, or light lunch!
